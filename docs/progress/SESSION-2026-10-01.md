### Why the tally says otherwise, and why it has not been edited

The cohort record above reads `Code SHAs: ['not recorded']` and the
tally reports `single_runtime_version: null` with
`sha_source: "versions.code_sha (single value; per-cycle SHAs not
recorded)"`. Its classification gives one reason — "the data feed was
not recorded" — and does not mention the runtime change.

That is not a fault in the rule. Per-cycle SHA recording shipped in the
**same redeploy** that occurred at 13:39:39, so the artifact running
when the session opened had no way to record it. The guard against
mid-session redeploys could not observe the mid-session redeploy that
installed the guard.

**The stored tally has not been modified.** Rewriting it would mean
editing a record to match a conclusion, which is the one thing a record
is for preventing. The classification above is an annotation alongside
it, with its sources named, and the disagreement is left visible.
