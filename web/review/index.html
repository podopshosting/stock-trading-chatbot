How this analysis works

The system combines several technical indicators, but indicators that measure similar behaviour are grouped together so they cannot artificially inflate conviction. A strong signal requires agreement across independent categories, not several readings of the same thing.

Trend — where price sits relative to its moving averages (MA crossover, golden/death cross).
Momentum — rate and direction of recent change (RSI, MACD, 10-day momentum).
Mean reversion — whether price is stretched from its range (Bollinger Bands).

The grouping was chosen from measured correlation between the signals' votes, not by intuition. When signals inside one group disagree, that group's influence is halved. When the groups themselves disagree, the result is HOLD.

This is analysis of past price data. It is not investment advice and it does not predict future prices.
